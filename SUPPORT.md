# Getting Help

Thanks for using Epix! Here's where to go depending on what you need.

## 🚨 Beware of scammers

Scam bots watch GitHub for words like "wallet", "seed" and "phrase". They reply to issues posing as Epix support or a "wallet recovery" service.

- Epix maintainers will **never** ask for your seed phrase, recovery phrase or private key.
- We will never send you to a "wallet validation", "sync" or "recovery" site, a Google Form, or ask you to message us on Telegram, WhatsApp or Discord DM.
- On GitHub, real maintainers show a **Member**, **Owner** or **Collaborator** badge next to their name.
- On Discord, we never DM you first. Ask for help in public channels.
- If you already shared your seed phrase, move your funds to a new wallet right away.

Report scam comments with the `...` menu, then **Report content**.

## 💬 Community chat

The fastest way to get help is the community [Discord](https://discord.gg/bF2GKHgrfv) — questions about running a node, building a xite, wallet setup, staking, and everything else.

## 🐛 Bug reports & feature requests

Open an issue on the relevant repository:

- Chain, tokenomics, RPC, precompiles → [EpixChain](https://github.com/EpixZone/EpixChain/issues)
- Node, browser, network, dashboard → [EpixNet](https://github.com/EpixZone/EpixNet/issues)
- Wallet → [epix-wallet](https://github.com/EpixZone/epix-wallet/issues)
- A specific app (Talk, Mail, Post, Blog, Wiki, Sites) → that xite's repository

Please use the issue templates and include versions, your platform, and reproduction steps.
